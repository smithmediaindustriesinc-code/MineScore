# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-20)

The first release.

- Disc Cutting Lathe: cut MP3, OGG, WAV, FLAC, MIDI and Note Block Studio files onto vinyl, copy records, load songs onto an MP3 player and press scores onto records. Runs on Forge Energy or on Create rotation.
- Vinyl Records: dyeable, trimmable at a smithing table, renamable at the lathe, wiped back to Blank Vinyl in a furnace.
- MP3 Player with a library, playlists, queue, shuffle and repeat, a 10-band EQ and Bluetooth playback through a speaker.
- Speakers: Subwoofer, Middle Speaker and Tweeter, each playing its own band, with daisy chaining, flush stacking, 3D cones that move with the music, and blowouts when overdriven.
- Hubs: Mixing Console, Channel Manager, Amp (master and line), Multi-Effects Unit, plus speaker wire in 12, 16 and 20 gauge, Cable Anchors and Wire Cutters.
- Multi-Effects Unit with six cards (drive and distortion, modulation, delay, reverb, amp and cabinet, dynamics and utility), an expression pedal and preset chips.
- Composer's Desk with a full score editor, live co-editing, playback through speakers, and MIDI / Note Block Studio / MineScore import and export.
- Vinyl Player, Vinyl Display and Music Cabinet.
- Room acoustics, sound through blocks and vinyl crackle.
- Redstone control and comparator output on every hub.
- Settings menu (Ctrl+M) with presets, import/export and server settings for operators.
- Built-in Modern Studio, Retro Hi-Fi and 16× resource packs, and Retro and Studio sound packs.
- The MineScore Manual, 19 advancements, crafting recipes with recipe-book unlocks, and particles (off by default).
- JEI, Jade and The One Probe support.
- Translated into German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Turkish, Swedish, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).

Coming in 1.1: a full dedicated-server test.
